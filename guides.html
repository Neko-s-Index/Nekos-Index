Safety, Verification & Online Safety

This index links to third-party websites and open-source projects. Always be cautious when downloading software from the internet, especially executable files and APKs. A few good habits and the right tools can protect you from malware, scams, and privacy leaks.

  • Stick to official sources: Download from the project's official site, GitHub releases, F-Droid, Flathub, or trusted package managers. Avoid random file-hosting sites and "cracked" software.
  • Scan everything: Upload files to VirusTotal before opening them. It runs the file through many antivirus engines and shows hidden payloads.
  • Keep real-time protection on: Use Malwarebytes, Avast, Bitdefender, or Windows Security with regular updates enabled.
  • Check the URL and certificate: Look for HTTPS, the correct domain, and a padlock icon. Scam sites often mimic real ones with typos or odd TLDs.
  • Keep everything updated: Keep your operating system, browser, and apps up to date to avoid known security holes.
  • Read before you install: Check user reviews, GitHub issues, Reddit threads, and the project's last update date. Old or unmaintained apps can be risky.
  • Run unknown apps in a sandbox: Tools like Sandboxie Plus or a disposable VM can isolate suspicious software from your main system.
  • Use a password manager: Bitwarden or KeePassXC keeps unique passwords and avoids phishing by not auto-filling on the wrong site.
  • Browser hardening: Use uBlock Origin, Privacy Badger, and disable JavaScript on untrusted sites with an extension like NoScript if needed.
  • Use a VPN or private DNS: These add privacy, but remember that no tool is 100% anonymous.
  • Back up your data: Use restic, Kopia, or a simple external drive so malware or mistakes can't wipe your files.

PC Apps — Getting Started

  • Package managers (Windows): Use Scoop, Chocolatey, or winget to install open-source apps from the command line.
  • Video players: Download mpv or VLC. Drag and drop a file or URL to play.
  • Torrenting: Use qBittorrent or Transmission. Always make sure your downloads are legal in your region.
  • Archives / utilities: 7-Zip and PeaZip handle zip, rar, 7z, and more.
  • Privacy / ad blocking: Try Brave, LibreWolf, uBlock Origin, or Privacy Badger.

Android Apps — Getting Started

  • Alternative app store: Install F-Droid or Droid-ify for open-source apps outside Google Play.
  • Play Store without Google: Aurora Store lets you download APKs anonymously.
  • Unknown sources: On Android, go to Settings > Security (or Apps) and allow installs from your file manager or browser for APK files.
  • YouTube apps: NewPipe and LibreTube are ad-free alternatives.
  • Video / anime / manga readers: Apps like Aniyomi, Mihon, and Aidoku use extensions; install the app first, then add extensions from within it.

iOS Apps — Getting Started

  • App Store: Many open-source apps such as VLC, Firefox, Brave, KeePassium, and Strongbox are on the App Store. Install them directly.
  • Sideloading with AltStore / SideStore: Apps like Yattee, iSH, and Delta are not in the App Store. Use AltStore or SideStore to sideload them on a non-jailbroken device. You will need to refresh them weekly unless you have a paid developer account.
  • Private DNS / VPN: Install a DNS profile for AdGuard DNS or NextDNS under Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > DNS. Or use a native app like Proton VPN or Mullvad VPN.
  • Content blockers: Use AdGuard for iOS or Safari extensions to block ads and trackers.
  • Passwords and 2FA: Use Bitwarden, KeePassium, or Strongbox for passwords, and keep 2FA enabled on important accounts.
  • Media and reading: VLC plays most files, Yattee is an Invidious/Piped client, and Aidoku is a manga reader. BookPlayer handles audiobooks.
  • Back up with iCloud or Nextcloud: Nextcloud and Proton Drive can back up files outside Apple's ecosystem.

VPN & DNS — Quick Setup

  • VPN clients: Download the official client for WireGuard or OpenVPN. Import a config file from your provider.
  • Private DNS (Android): Settings > Network & Internet > Private DNS > enter a hostname such as dns.adguard-dns.com or your NextDNS address.
  • DNS on PC / router: Use AdGuard DNS, NextDNS, Quad9, or run your own Pi-hole.
  • Mesh / self-hosted VPNs: Tailscale, ZeroTier, and Twingate are easy to set up for private networks.

Streaming & Media

  • Multi-platform servers: Jellyfin and Kodi are free media centers that work on PC, Android, iOS, and TV.
  • CloudStream: Install the app, then add extensions from the community to get content sources.
  • Stremio: Install, then use the built-in add-ons or community add-ons. Pair with Real-Debrid for smoother streaming if allowed in your region.
  • Music / audiobooks: VLC, Jellyfin, or BookPlayer on iOS handle most formats.
