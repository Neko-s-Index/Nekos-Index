Neko's Index is committed to user privacy. This page explains what data is collected or stored when you use this site.

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or account details.
  • We do not use tracking cookies or analytics.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Local Storage

This site uses your browser's localStorage to save preferences locally on your device. This includes:

  • Theme preference (light/dark)
  • Category collapsed/expanded states
  • Your favorited items
  • Whether you have accepted the disclaimer
  • Settings toggles (IP warning, beta notice, compact mode)

This data never leaves your browser. It is not transmitted to us or any server.

IP Address Notice

When you load this site, your public IP address may be fetched from a public service (api.ipify.org) and displayed in the top banner. This is only for awareness and privacy education. The IP is not stored or transmitted anywhere.

External Links

This site contains links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before using them.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
